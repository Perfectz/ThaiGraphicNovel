# Bangkok Rift — words become power

## Intent
User requests a roughly two-minute (never over three-minute) game showcase explaining the new pronunciation-powered turn-based combat. Show GPT 6 building functionality: Blender assets, combat development, prerecorded voices, supplied character models, actual gameplay and Bangkok story.

## Direction
Show the working game, with a cinematic brass/gold and deep teal visual identity. Narrated 16:9 showcase, actual browser footage and Blender renders; brief explanatory typography and a readable gameplay demonstration. Supplied character models are credited as integrated assets, not claimed as generated by GPT 6. Label synthetic test input and experimental pronunciation assessment truthfully. No fabricated learner improvement or claims of validated Thai grading.

## Execution
The user authorized creation of the finished video. Proceed autonomously through storyboard, captured footage, narration, verification and final render. Defaults: 1920x1080, English voiceover, captions, approximate 120-second length. These are routine production decisions based on the requested showcase, not extra approval gates. No further interview required.

## Assets
Use the local game's footage and authored Blender models/scripts. Existing OpenAI-generated character audio and existing music are preferred. Generate English narration with the project's authorized OpenAI key in process memory only. Keep source/capture evidence and final video separate from the game deployment.

## Delivery
Verified MP4, thumbnail/contact sheet, editable HyperFrames source, and updated playable game. No promise of native-phone microphone verification or Thai-speaker calibration.
