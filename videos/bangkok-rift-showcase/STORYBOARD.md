# Bangkok Rift showcase

Duration: 134 seconds. 1920×1080. Actual staged game footage; synthetic Thai test input with live Realtime results.

- 0–18s: WORDS BECOME POWER
  What if practising Thai could power your next attack? This is Bangkok Rift: a playable, three dimensional RPG, built through an ongoing collaboration with GPT six. Explore Bangkok, meet its people, and turn useful language into action.

- 18–36s: A CITY. A COMPANION. A QUEST.
  Your adventure begins in a Sukhumvit hotel. Travel with Su through park paths, lantern lit streets, and the riverside. Conversations, discoveries, and story battles give each destination a purpose, as you work to restore the light of the last ferry.

- 36–54s: FROM CODE TO CRAFT
  Behind the streets are original Blender assets, authored through Python: shophouses, an elevated railway, riverboats, and lanterns. GPT six connects that geometry to lighting, movement, collisions, and the world you can actually explore.

- 54–70s: CHARACTERS WITH A VOICE
  Supplied character models become your animated party. Prerecorded OpenAI voices bring dialogue and Thai examples into the adventure. Listen normally, hear a phrase slowly, then record yourself and compare.

- 70–96s: SAY IT. POWER IT. CAST IT.
  Combat is turn based. Choose an ability and a target, then read what you mean in English before speaking Thai. First Light now offers experimental pronunciation power. Realtime assesses the actual recording, and the game shows how that rating changes your attack. Your turn waits for your decision.

- 96–116s: A BETTER ATTEMPT. A STRONGER MOVE.
  Ask Su for a short explanation, or ask a question aloud. Record a coached retry and keep your better result. Only your confirmation releases the attack. These are experimental AI estimates; Thai speaker calibration is still ahead.

- 116–134s: LEARN THROUGH ADVENTURE
  The same adventure supports touch controls on a phone. Explore, prepare your party, practise, and return stronger. Bangkok Rift brings asset creation, story, voice, and combat together in one playable project. Try the build, and help shape what comes next.
