# Visual direction
Canvas: #071d27. Ink: #f5e8c7. Accent: #e5bd72. Secondary: #8fd5c9.
Typography: Georgia for large elegant chapter headings, Segoe UI for captions and precise supporting text.
1920x1080. Preserve real footage; do not reconstruct gameplay with fake UI. Letterbox footage inside clean brass framing only where needed for readable annotation.
Opening and closing: large editorial typography over cinematic city footage. Craft: model renders at right, actual script excerpt at left. Combat: full-width real recording with quiet labels and generous space for the game UI. Captions occupy the bottom 100px, never cover critical gameplay. 24px minimum small labels, 32px captions, 66-100px headlines.
Motion: restrained mask reveal from installed lt-mask-reveal primitive, slow image scale, lateral chapter transitions, crisp progress rule. No decorative dashboard cards, no invented performance claims.
