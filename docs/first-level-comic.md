# Isekai Thai Quest: Level 1 Comic Book Script

## Issue Title

**Hotel Lobby Basics**

## Logline

Patrick wakes up inside the glowing lobby of the Chao Phraya Star Hotel after falling through a Bangkok rift. Before he can survive the city outside, Su turns the lobby into a safe tutorial battle where every polite Thai phrase becomes a move, every clear pronunciation builds Understanding, and asking for water restores Courage.

## Main Cast

- **Patrick**: Confused, newly transported, trying to stay brave.
- **Su**: Mentor, guide, pronunciation coach, and the first tutorial opponent.
- **The Bangkok Rift**: A violet tear of magic pulsing through the hotel lobby.

## Visual Tone

Bright isekai comic energy with thick ink lines, warm hotel golds, red carpet, blue runner tiles, and a violet rift glow. The lobby should feel safe but magical, like a training arena disguised as a hotel entrance.

## Page 1: The Lobby That Should Not Exist

### Panel 1

**Wide shot.** Patrick lies on polished marble in the Chao Phraya Star Hotel lobby. A violet rift flickers behind him like a torn elevator door. Suitcases hover a few inches off the floor.

**Caption:** Level 1: Chao Phraya Star Hotel.

**Patrick:** Ow... this is not my apartment.

### Panel 2

**Close on Patrick.** He props himself up, still dizzy. The lobby sign glows above him. The carpet points deeper into the hotel like a quest path.

**Patrick:** Why is the hotel lobby glowing?

### Panel 3

**Action panel.** Su rushes in from the side of the lobby, one hand raised, gold light trailing behind her.

**Su:** Patrick! You are awake. Do not panic.

### Panel 4

**Two-shot.** Su kneels beside Patrick while the Bangkok rift pulses behind them.

**Su:** You fell through the Bangkok rift and landed in the lobby of the Chao Phraya Star Hotel.

**Patrick:** That explains nothing and somehow makes it worse.

## Page 2: First Kind Of Magic

### Panel 1

**Medium shot.** Su helps Patrick stand. A translucent battle grid appears across the marble, carpet, wood, and runner tiles.

**Su:** Before you step outside, you need your first kind of magic.

**Patrick:** Please say sword.

### Panel 2

**Hero panel.** Su points toward Patrick. Thai letters, romanization, and English meaning appear in floating comic captions.

**Su:** Polite Thai.

**Floating caption:** Hello. Sawatdee khrap.

### Panel 3

**Patrick squints at the floating words.**

**Patrick:** So the first boss is... learning how to talk like a normal polite person?

### Panel 4

**Su smiles, calm and serious.**

**Su:** Exactly. Clear pronunciation builds Understanding. Courage keeps you standing. You will say ten basics before you leave this lobby.

**UI caption:** Objective: Complete 10 phrases.

## Page 3: The Trial Begins

### Panel 1

**Splash panel.** The lobby transforms into a safe Thai Basics Trial. Su stands across from Patrick like a mentor in a training duel. The violet rift dims but watches from the back wall.

**Battle title:** Su - Thai Basics Trial.

**Su:** Say each phrase into the AI mic. Listen. Try again. Improve.

### Panel 2

**Close on Patrick clutching a small notebook that appears in his hand.**

**Caption:** Starter weapon acquired: Traveler's Notebook.

**Patrick:** This is my weapon?

**Su:** Your words are.

### Panel 3

**UI style panel.** Bars appear beside Patrick: Courage, Understanding, and Super.

**Caption:** Courage starts low. Understanding grows phrase by phrase. The Super charges after three successful phrases.

### Panel 4

**Patrick takes a breath.**

**Patrick:** Sawatdee khrap.

**Su:** Good. Again, with confidence.

## Page 4: First Contact

### Panel 1

**Training montage panel.** Three phrase cards orbit Patrick.

**Card 1:** Hello - sawatdee khrap.

**Card 2:** My name is Patrick - phom chue Patrick khrap.

**Card 3:** Nice to meet you - yin dee tee dai roo jak khrap.

### Panel 2

**Patrick bows awkwardly with a wai.**

**Patrick:** My name is Patrick.

**Su:** Better. Keep the polite ending. It tells people you are trying.

### Panel 3

**The rift spits a little spark toward Patrick. He flinches, but the phrase cards shield him.**

**Caption:** In this level, language is defense before it is attack.

### Panel 4

**Su gives a teacherly thumbs-up.**

**Su:** First Contact cleared.

**UI caption:** Understanding 3/10. Super ready: Wai of Clarity.

## Page 5: Polite Repair

### Panel 1

**Comedic panel.** Patrick backs into a floating luggage cart.

**Patrick:** Sorry! Excuse me!

**Phrase caption:** Sorry / excuse me - kho thot khrap.

### Panel 2

**Su steadies the cart with one hand.**

**Su:** Mistakes are part of the lesson. Repair them politely.

**Phrase caption:** Thank you - khop khun khrap.

### Panel 3

**Split panel.** Su asks a question. Patrick answers once correctly, once incorrectly.

**Su:** Are you ready?

**Patrick:** Chai khrap. Yes.

**Second caption:** Mai chai khrap. No / not correct.

### Panel 4

**The battle grid glows brighter. Patrick looks less panicked.**

**Caption:** Polite Repair teaches gratitude, apology, and simple yes/no control.

**UI caption:** Understanding 7/10.

## Page 6: First Needs

### Panel 1

**Market-vision panel.** The lobby briefly overlays with future Bangkok scenes: a desk, a shop, a hallway sign.

**Su:** Now the survival phrases. Ask price. Find the bathroom. Ask for water.

### Panel 2

**Patrick holds up the Traveler's Notebook like a menu.**

**Patrick:** How much is it?

**Phrase caption:** Raa khaa tao rai khrap.

### Panel 3

**Patrick points toward a glowing hotel sign.**

**Patrick:** Where is the bathroom?

**Phrase caption:** Hong nam yoo tee nai khrap.

### Panel 4

**The rift pressure rises. Patrick's Courage bar drops. His knees wobble.**

**Patrick:** I think the lobby is fighting back.

**Su:** Then ask for what you need.

## Page 7: The Heal Phrase

### Panel 1

**Close on Patrick.** Sweat on his brow, Courage nearly empty. The mic icon glows.

**Caption:** Heal phrase unlocked.

**Su:** Say it clearly.

### Panel 2

**Patrick speaks with real effort.**

**Patrick:** Phom hiw naam khrap.

**Phrase caption:** I am thirsty.

### Panel 3

**A bottle of water materializes in a burst of blue light. Courage surges.**

**UI caption:** Courage restored.

**Patrick:** The magic water listened to me.

**Su:** You listened to yourself.

### Panel 4

**Patrick stands upright, notebook open, rift wind blowing through the pages.**

**Caption:** The first level teaches that asking clearly is power.

## Page 8: Wai Of Clarity

### Panel 1

**Su raises both hands.** Three phrase seals orbit Patrick: Hello, Thank you, I am thirsty.

**Su:** Your Super Move is charged.

**Patrick:** Finally. Sword?

**Su:** Wai of Clarity.

### Panel 2

**Patrick performs a wai.** A clean wave of light crosses the lobby. The rift stabilizes. The tiles stop shaking.

**SFX:** KRA-WAI!

**Caption:** Spend full charge to restore Courage and shake loose hesitation.

### Panel 3

**The battle UI flashes complete.**

**UI caption:** Hotel Lobby Basics complete. Reward: Traveler's Notebook.

**Su:** You have your first ten phrases. Now you can step toward the front desk.

### Panel 4

**Final page image.** Patrick and Su face the glowing route from Lobby to Front Desk. The rift is still there, but Patrick is no longer on the floor.

**Patrick:** So Bangkok survival starts with hello?

**Su:** With hello, thank you, sorry, and asking for water before you collapse.

**Caption:** Next route: Front Desk Check-in.

## Back Matter: Level 1 Phrase List

1. Hello - sawatdee khrap.
2. My name is Patrick - phom chue Patrick khrap.
3. Nice to meet you - yin dee tee dai roo jak khrap.
4. Thank you - khop khun khrap.
5. Sorry / excuse me - kho thot khrap.
6. Yes - chai khrap.
7. No / not correct - mai chai khrap.
8. How much is it? - raa khaa tao rai khrap.
9. Where is the bathroom? - hong nam yoo tee nai khrap.
10. I am thirsty - phom hiw naam khrap.

## Gameplay Notes Reflected In The Comic

- The first level is **Hotel Lobby Basics** in the **Chao Phraya Star Hotel**.
- Su is the tutorial mentor and safe first battle opponent.
- Patrick begins confused after the Bangkok rift drops him into the lobby.
- The core loop is voice practice: speak each phrase, receive a pronunciation judgment, and build Understanding.
- Courage is the pressure meter. The thirst phrase acts as the healing moment.
- Passing phrases charges **Wai of Clarity**.
- Completing the level points the player toward **Front Desk Check-in**.
