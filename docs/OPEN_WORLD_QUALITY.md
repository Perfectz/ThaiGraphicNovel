The goal is a travelable open-world Thai-learning adventure, beginning in a Sukhumvit hotel, with distinct Bangkok places and the quality of strong learning games and JRPGs. This is the completion standard, not a description of whatever happens to exist today. The goal remains active until the rendered game and played learning loop meet it.

## Reference games and what they establish

These are reference strengths, not a claim that publisher descriptions prove learning efficacy or that our game already matches their quality.

- [Dragon Quest XI — official towns, fields, dungeons and camps](https://www.dq11.jp/stage/index.html): towns have people, information and useful services; routes reward exploration; camps offer recovery. Our neighbourhoods must function as places to explore and revisit, not labels on a level picker.
- [Expedition 33 — official gameplay introduction](https://www.expedition33.com/post/your-first-look-at-gameplay-from-clair-obscur-expedition-33): deliberate party turns coexist with reactive defense. Our word abilities must remain tactical, with readable enemy intentions and meaningful companion actions.
- [Influent](https://playinfluent.com/): player-directed discovery inside a navigable environment. Our Thai should attach to things the player wants to do in the world.
- [Lingo Legend](https://www.lingolegend.com/home): relevant language categories, RPG progression and scheduled review. Our return sessions need useful recall and new goals, not an endlessly repeatable quiz or walking time counted as instruction.
