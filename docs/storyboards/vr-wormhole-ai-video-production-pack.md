# VR Wormhole AI Video Production Pack

Use these assets to generate the intro video for the Thai Quest VR wormhole sequence. The pack keeps Patrick and Su aligned with the shipped 3D model designs while presenting three alternate 4-frame shot plans.

## Storyboard Options

1. [First-person portal pull](./vr-wormhole-intro-storyboard-v1-first-person.png)
   - Best for VR presence.
   - Viewer hands remain visible as the portal forms around the player.
   - Use this when the video should feel like the player is being carried through the rift.

2. [Character-led rescue](./vr-wormhole-intro-storyboard-v2-character-led.png)
   - Best for Patrick and Su performance.
   - Su stabilizes Patrick, then both are pulled into the wormhole.
   - Use this when the video should emphasize the duo relationship.

3. [Lobby-to-cosmos transformation](./vr-wormhole-intro-storyboard-v3-transformation.png)
   - Best for transition-heavy generation.
   - Hotel pieces become cosmic structures: chandelier to stars, elevator doors to portal ribs, marble to rift rings.
   - Use this when the video should emphasize magical environment transformation.

## Continuity Rules

- Patrick: bald, black sunglasses, trimmed goatee, red jacket, black shirt, fingerless gloves, dark pants, black boots with red accents.
- Su: blonde high bun, coral-pink jacket with pale cyber-magic markings, black outfit, crossbody bag, black boots, cyan/coral/gold guide magic.
- Keep the game art style: polished anime RPG key art, crisp silhouettes, saturated Bangkok fantasy color, and toon-rendered 3D model proportions.
- Avoid photorealism, gritty movie realism, franchise parody, speech bubbles, readable UI text, and extra main characters.

## Motion Notes

- Camera should feel VR-stable: forward drift, gentle banking, no violent spin.
- Rift motion should wrap around the viewer, not appear as a flat portal ahead.
- Time debris should pass at multiple depths: shoulder height, eye level, overhead, and underfoot.
- Su's magic should act as the stabilizing visual line: coral/cyan shield rings, gold guide threads, and soft tether arcs.
- Patrick should feel pulled along ahead of the viewer: reaching back, bracing, drifting sideways, then landing hard.

## Suggested Video Structure

Use a 12-18 second intro:

1. Lobby thread forms around viewer, 2-3 seconds.
2. Patrick and Su are pulled into the rift, 3-4 seconds.
3. Time debris and era split, 4-6 seconds.
4. Cosmic cathedral peak, 2-3 seconds.
5. Soft return into the hotel lobby, 2-3 seconds.

The three storyboard options can be mixed: start with the first-person opening, use the character-led rescue for the pull, then use the transformation board for the lobby-to-cosmos transitions.
