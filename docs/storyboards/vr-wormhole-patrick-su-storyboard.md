# VR Wormhole Storyboard

Visual board: [vr-wormhole-thai-quest-storyboard-v2.png](./vr-wormhole-thai-quest-storyboard-v2.png)

## Intent

Create a VR-first wormhole sequence where the player feels carried alongside the game's Patrick and Su through time, memory, and space. Patrick is the bald player character in the red rift jacket, sunglasses, and goatee. Su is the main female AI tutor princess companion in the coral cyber-magic jacket. The portal should not read as a flat doorway ahead of the player. It should wrap the player in 360 degrees, with discoverable details above, below, beside, and behind.

## Panels

1. **Hotel Pull**
   - Near-dark hotel lobby with faint chandelier, elevator doors, ticking clock, and distant ambient sound.
   - A thin golden thread circles the player space, suggesting the portal is forming around the viewer while Su steadies the spell.

2. **360 Break**
   - Wormhole opens around the player.
   - Stars streak in all directions while Patrick travels just ahead, reaching backward as if trying to stay anchored.
   - Su's coral-and-cyan guide magic trails beside him, keeping the viewer oriented.

3. **Personal Time Debris**
   - Clock face, hotel key, neon sign, pages, and small galaxies drift close to the player.
   - Objects should pass at shoulder, eye, overhead, and floor depth to reward looking around while Patrick and Su move through the debris.

4. **Weightless Bank**
   - The wormhole pushes Patrick sideways.
   - Su reaches toward him with a stabilizing ribbon of magic.
   - Camera motion stays smooth: forward drift, gentle banking, no violent roll or snap spin.

5. **Era Split**
   - Left side opens into an ancient temple corridor stretching into infinity.
   - Right side flashes with a futuristic city.
   - Below, hotel marble appears briefly before dissolving into stars as Patrick and Su float between eras.

6. **Origin Crack**
   - Looking behind reveals the original world shrinking into a distant glowing crack.
   - Directional whispers and faint era-voice traces imply sound from different times while Su points back toward the origin.

7. **Cathedral Peak**
   - Wormhole becomes a cosmic cathedral with giant rings, clock hands, constellations, and orbiting magic symbols.
   - Ghostly silhouettes from Patrick's game-world memories appear at different depths, some looking toward Patrick and some toward the viewer.
   - Su shields the viewer with layered coral-and-cyan cyber-magic markings.

8. **Soft Return**
   - The wormhole folds into the hotel lobby instead of cutting hard.
   - Stars become chandelier crystals, cosmic dust becomes lobby dust, and gold rings become the marble floor pattern.
   - Patrick lands ahead of the player, breathing hard, while Su lands beside him and lets the last magic trails fade.
   - They realize they have not just arrived somewhere, but somewhen.

## VR Direction

- Keep the player camera stable and embodied: smooth forward motion, gentle banking turns, and brief weightless float moments.
- Avoid violent spin, abrupt cuts, or disorienting camera acceleration.
- Use layered spatial audio: lobby sounds behind, whispers from era-specific directions, and a stretching room tone during portal formation.
- Preserve 360 detail density: every direction should have at least one readable visual element during the wormhole peak.
- After landing, keep the lobby calm but haunted: faint galaxy in chandelier glass, elevator reflections showing space for a few seconds, and the front desk clock ticking backward once before stopping.
